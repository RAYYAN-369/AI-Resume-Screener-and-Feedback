Everything one scan produces

One upload, fifteen feedback signals.

Every scan runs the same pipeline against your resume and the job description grouped below by what each check is actually for.

Matching & scoring

How closely the resume lines up with the job description, in numbers.

ATS score

Keyword-matching score built from a curated technical skills list, the same style of check an automated screener runs.

Overall resume score

The model's holistic read on resume quality and clarity, evidence, and presentation, not just keywords.

Matched skills

The exact technical and soft skills the resume already demonstrates that the job description also asks for.

Missing skills

Requirements the job description names that the resume doesn't currently show evidence of.

Keyword recommendations

ATS-relevant terms worth adding to the resume, drawn from language the posting actually uses.

Resume content feedback

Section-by-section notes on what's written, not just what's missing.

Grammar review

Wording and grammar issues flagged directly in the resume text.

Formatting feedback

Consistency and readability notes — spacing, headings, bullet structure the kind of thing parsers trip on.

Experience feedback

Suggestions for strengthening how work experience is described and evidenced.

Education feedback

What's missing or underdeveloped in the education section CGPA, coursework, honors.

Projects feedback

Notes on making project entries read as evidence , outcomes and links, not just titles.

Strengths & weaknesses

A direct list of what's working in the resume and what's holding it back.

Guidance & next steps

Where the scan turns into something actionable.

AI summary

An 80–120 word professional summary tying the score, strengths, and weaknesses together.

Interview readiness

A direct read on whether the candidate looks interview-ready based on what's evidenced.

Improvement suggestions

At least eight concrete, resume-specific suggestions not generic career advice.

Trust & privacy

What happens to your files, in plain terms.

Local AI, no paid API

Analysis runs through Ollama on the machine hosting the app , no external AI service call.

Secure file handling

Only PDF and DOCX are accepted; files are used for the scan you run.

Structured, validated output

Every response is checked against a schema before it reaches the results page no partial or malformed data shown.
