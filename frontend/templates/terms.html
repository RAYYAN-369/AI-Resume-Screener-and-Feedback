What this tool is

AI Resume Scanner compares an uploaded resume against a job description, computes an ATS-style match score, and generates AI feedback using a locally hosted language model. It's built for guidance and self-review.

What this tool is not

  • It is not a hiring decision, an official ATS, or a guarantee of how a specific employer's systems will treat your resume.
  • It is not legal, career, or immigration advice.
  • Scores can vary between runs, since the underlying model is probabilistic — treat the specific feedback as more reliable than the exact number.

Acceptable use

  • Upload only resumes and job descriptions you have the right to share.
  • Don't use the upload fields to submit malicious files, scripts, or content unrelated to resume screening.
  • Don't attempt to use the tool to identify, profile, or discriminate against real individuals outside of your own resume review.
